About

I teach English for the CEFR Multilevel exam and IELTS (my own score: band 8). I run the Teacher Azimjon channels on YouTube and Telegram, where students practise real speaking, listening, reading and writing tasks.

Before teaching, I studied computer science at Sejong University in Seoul and worked as a web developer. Today I combine both: I am a master's student in Information Technologies in Education at Karshi State Technical University, and I design the platforms I use with my students.

Research

My research looks at gamification in education: what happens to student practice when a learning platform adds points, levels and leaderboards, why the effect fades, and how game elements can adapt to each learner.

  • 2026
    Gamified web platform for exam practice
    Conference paper on a points-and-leaderboard platform used by English learners preparing for the Multilevel exam.
  • In progress
    The novelty effect in gamified learning
    Usage data showing a sharp rise and gradual fall in practice after points were introduced, and a design for adaptive gamification.
  • In progress
    Gamification in teaching programming: a systematic review
    Studies from 2015–2025 on game elements in IT and programming courses.
